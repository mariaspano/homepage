About

Hi, I'm Maria

I am a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor (RTDB) in Social Statistics (STAT-03/B) at the University of Naples Federico II, Department of Economics and Statistics, where I also earned my Ph.D. in Statistics. I hold the National Scientific Qualification as Associate Professor (13/D3, valid until 2034).

My research focuses on quantitative methods for text and data analysis, with an emphasis on computational approaches to social science and bibliometrics. Since 2017 I have been part of the development team of bibliometrix; I co-develop TALL and themescopeR, and I am a member of the academic spin-off K-Synth and of the VRD Lab for research and teaching evaluation.

Since 2024 I have been a permanent member of the Scientific Committee of JADT (International Conference on Statistical Analysis of Textual Data). I chaired the organising committee of JADT 2022 in Naples and served on the organising committee of CLADAG 2025. I took part in the PRIN 2022 projects SciK-Health and The value of scientific production for patient care in Academic Health Science Centres, and I am a member of the Andalusian FEDER project C-ING-165-UGR23 with the University of Granada. At DiSES I sit on the Visiting Committee (since 2021) and the Communication and Social Media Committee (since 2026).

Research interests

What I work on

Text Mining

Statistical analysis of textual data, topic and theme extraction, text summarisation and framing detection.

Sentiment Analysis

Opinion mining on social media, online reviews and student feedback, including geo-referenced sentiment.

Science Mapping

Bibliometrics, conceptual and thematic evolution of research fields, evaluation of scientific production.

Document Clustering

Data mining, clustering validation and network-based dimensionality reduction for large document collections.
